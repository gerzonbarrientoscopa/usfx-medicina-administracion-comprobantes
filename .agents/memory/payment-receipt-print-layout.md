---
name: Payment receipt print layout
description: The required paper size and top-aligned sizing behavior for payment receipts.
---

**Rule:** Payment receipts print on portrait US Letter paper, use the full printable width at the top of the page, and keep a 5.5-inch minimum receipt height. Additional concept rows extend the content naturally; do not switch to half-letter paper or vertically center/shrink the receipt.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that one to three concept rows occupy the upper half of a Letter sheet and that extra rows grow only as needed, matching their approved reference image.

**How to apply:** Keep print page size fixed at Letter and preserve top alignment, width, and visible receipt framing whenever payment receipt styles or print handlers change.
